# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on the
work. Welcome people regardless of background or experience. Give technical
feedback about ideas and evidence, not about a person's character.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing their effects; and
- prioritizing the safety and usefulness of the community.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and sustained
personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security-reporting
channel when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as privately and
impartially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces, as well as when someone publicly represents the project.
